# Deviation log

The Part-2 brief (item 2i) says: "Deviations from the submitted document should be documented and explained during the final demo." This log lists every way the build differs from the **submitted** documents: SRS v1.1, SAD v1.0 and Software Test Plan v1.0.

Each pull request that introduces a difference adds a row. The SRS v11 working draft referred to in code comments is the team's revised SRS, which supplies the detailed rules that SRS v1.1 leaves open.

**Status:**
- **Temporary**: closed by a later issue.
- **Permanent**: a deliberate decision, explained here.
- **Open**: still needs a team decision.

| D-02 | AC-HMS-F-002 (and issue #2 as first written): a matching phone number blocks a new UHID. | A shared phone number is allowed after staff review: the screen lists the existing patients with that number, and staff choose "same patient" (no new record) or "different person" (new UHID). The submitted behaviour is one setting: `PatientPolicy::phoneMatchAllowsNewPatient = false`. | The submitted requirement contradicts its own acceptance criterion: the requirement text says "warn and require explicit confirmation", the criterion says "block". | D-04 | HMS-F-001: "contact details" (undefined). | One Indian mobile number, accepted as 10 digits or with 0, 91 or +91 in front, stored as the 10 digits. **Required** (team decision): for a child or an elderly patient, a family member's or carer's number is entered. Only when neither the patient nor anyone with them has a phone, staff choose "no phone available" and confirm it; this is stored on the record (`no_phone_confirmed`) and shown as "No phone (confirmed at registration)". A made-up number is never entered. | A definition was needed. Most patients are reachable through someone, but a hospital cannot turn away a patient who has no phone, and an invented number would match other patients and send messages to a stranger. Migration 1 is merged and not edited, so migration 3 adds the flag and triggers that enforce "a number or a confirmed no phone" for new and changed records. Records saved before migration 3 without a number are kept and shown as "Not recorded (older record)". | Permanent | #2 follow-up |

| D-24 | HMS-F-004: search by Patient ID, name or phone number. | Two additions.
